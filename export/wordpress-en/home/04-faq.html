Dedicated servers are isolated; for stricter requirements, our Private AI (air-gapped) option keeps data inside your own systems. Details are agreed during the assessment.

Rack rental (colocation), private cloud, backup and disaster recovery, connectivity, and hosting GPU infrastructure for AI services.

Yes. Rent by the U, half rack or full rack; Vu Hai helps with installation, cabling and monitoring.
