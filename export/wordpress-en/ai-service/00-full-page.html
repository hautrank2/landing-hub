01How is renting an AI server different from regular cloud?

You get a dedicated GPU server with a clear configuration, unshared resources and a team that helps you deploy your AI services — not just a virtual machine.

Dedicated servers are isolated; for stricter requirements, our Private AI (air-gapped) option keeps data inside your own systems. Details are agreed during the assessment.

It depends on the model (hourly, monthly or dedicated cluster) and GPU type. Vu Hai quotes based on your actual configuration and term — leave your details for a consultation.

04Does Vu Hai help deploy AI models?

Yes. Our team sets up environments, builds chatbots/RAG and inference APIs, and integrates them with systems such as VAN-Logistic or ERP.
