How it works

From assessment to operation in five steps

  1. 01

    Needs assessment

    We discuss device count, power draw, bandwidth and the availability you need.

  2. 02

    Solution design

    We propose rack placement, power, cooling and network cabling.

  3. 03

    Installation & cabling

    Equipment is moved in, the network configured, power and temperature checked.

  4. 04

    Acceptance

    We walk through a checklist with you before go-live.

  5. 05

    Operations & support

    24/7 monitoring, technical support and resource adjustments as your needs change.
